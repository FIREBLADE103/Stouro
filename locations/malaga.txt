Notes
- Site reference point: a sourced coordinate (lat, lng) for the site as a whole. It is NOT an entrance and must not be used as one.
- Approximate approach point: a sourced but approximate coordinate for the area around an entrance. It is NOT a verified doorway pin.
- Verified visitor entrance: a sourced coordinate for the actual public entrance, or UNVERIFIED. No entrance coordinates have been verified yet.
- Address: marked "(confirmed)" where it matches the source; otherwise it is from general knowledge and unchecked.
- Nearest street and approach come from general knowledge and have not been checked against a source, unless an access note says otherwise.
- Source: URL(s) backing the site point, address or access note.
